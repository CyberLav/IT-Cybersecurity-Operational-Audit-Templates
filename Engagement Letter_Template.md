### 1. Introduction & Objective

For each of these risks Internal Audit has preliminary identified the various potential root causes in order to determine which business process should be included in this audit. The identified business processes are as follows:

* Governance   
* Access and privileged account management   
* Patch and vulnerability management   
* Threat monitoring   
* Reporting and consequence actions   
* Training and awareness

  
### 3. Methodology 
The audit will cover all activities and controls related to the processes mentioned above. Internal Audit will perform a detailed analysis to identify the possible causes of each risk and validate the design and operating effectiveness of the key controls mitigating these risks. The adopted strategy will include the following elements:

* Discussions with key management, staff and the Managed Service partner to identify the causes of key risks and determine the nature of the various processes;  
* Identifying, documenting and executing walkthroughs of various business processes;  
* Identification and evaluation of key controls, as well as an overall assessment of the control environment; and  
* Assessment of the operating effectiveness of identified key controls on a sample basis.
