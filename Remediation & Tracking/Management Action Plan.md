### **Achieving an Agreed Management Action Plan**

The process to finalize a **Management Action Plan (MAP)** is a collaborative negotiation between the auditor and management. The goal is to move from audit observation to business-owned resolution.

1. **Drafting & Validation**: Once findings are identified, draft the initial remediation recommendation. Socialize this draft with the relevant process owners to ensure the proposed solution is technically feasible and operationally realistic.  
2. **Negotiation & Alignment**: Review the recommendations with leadership. During this phase, you may negotiate the **Remediation Approach**:  
   * **Mitigated**: Management will implement a control to reduce risk to an acceptable level.  
   * **Transferred**: The risk is shifted to a third party (e.g., insurance or outsourcing).  
   * **Accepted**: Management acknowledges the risk and decides to take no action (requires formal sign-off due to potential exposure).  
3. **Ownership & Accountability**: Identify the specific "Control Owner"—the individual with the authority and resources to implement the change.  
4. **Deadline Setting**: Agree on a firm Target Date. This must be realistic to avoid unnecessary extensions, but urgent enough to protect the business.  
5. **Formal Sign-Off**: The final MAP is signed off by management, creating a binding commitment to remediation that will be monitored until validation.
