Risk Register Matrix
Formula Engine: IR = I × L | RR = IR × (CE / 5)
I • Impact Rating

1: Low | 2: Minor | 3: Moderate
4: High | 5: Critical

L • Likelihood Rating

1: Low | 2: Minor | 3: Moderate
4: High | 5: Critical

IR • Inherent Risk

Formula: I × L
Max scale value is 25.

CE • Control Effectiveness

1: Strong (80% mitigation)
5: Absent/Ineffective (0% mitigation)

RR • Residual Risk / Priority

Formula: IR × (CE / 5)
High: ≥12 | Med: 6-11.9 | Low: <6
