### **Audit Report Ratings**

| Rating | Definition |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Strong** | Controls are robust, fully automated, and operate effectively to mitigate all significant risks. |

| **Unsatisfactory** | Critical control failures exist; the current state poses a high, unmitigated risk to the organization. |
