# IT Cybersecurity Operational Audit

This structured framework outlines a professional lifecycle for IT and Cybersecurity audit engagements, designed to transform technical assessments into actionable risk intelligence.

### **Planning & Scoping**

This phase establishes the "rules of engagement" to ensure the audit remains focused on high-priority business risks.

●       **Audit Engagement Memo**: Formalize the project’s objectives, scope, and legal boundaries to ensure stakeholder alignment.  
●       **Risk Assessment & Scoping**: Utilize a ranking tool to evaluate the risk profile of systems and processes, prioritizing efforts toward the organization's most critical assets.

### **Fieldwork & Execution**

This phase serves as the engine of the audit, transitioning from planning to the hands-on verification of internal controls.

●       **Risk Control Matrix (RCM)**: Map specific controls to technical testing steps to provide a clear roadmap for verifying design and operating effectiveness.  
●       **Evidence Request Tracker**: Manage the flow of documentation, ensuring that all logs, configurations, and interview artifacts are tracked and accounted for.  
●       **Testing Working Papers**: Utilize a standardized format to document the results of each control test, establishing a defensible audit trail.

### **Reporting & Findings**

This phase translates technical observations into language that supports informed business decision-making.

●       **Finding Log Classification**: Apply a standardized matrix (Impact × Likelihood) to objectively categorize the severity of each identified risk.  
●       **Final Audit Report**: Present technical findings in a clean, executive-friendly layout that emphasizes business impact and clear remediation pathways.

### **Remediation & Tracking**

The engagement concludes by driving the resolution of identified security gaps.

●       **Management Action Plan**: Track remediation progress by assigning clear ownership and firm deadlines to each management-approved action item.  
●       **Follow-up Status**: Monitor the implementation of controls to validate that security gaps are effectively closed and risks are properly treated.
